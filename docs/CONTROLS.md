# Controls

| Key | Action |
|---|---|
| A or Left Arrow | Move left |
| D or Right Arrow | Move right |
| Space | Jump (press again in the air to double jump) |
| Esc | Pause or resume (in menus: go back) |
| Enter | Confirm a menu choice, or continue after a level |
| R | Restart after game over, or after the victory screen |
| M | Sound on or off |
| F11 | Fullscreen on or off |
| Mouse | Hover and click menu buttons, drag settings sliders |

## Menus

- Up and Down arrows (or W and S) move the selection.
- Left and Right arrows change a volume slider.
- Locked levels in Level Select are greyed out until you finish the level before them.

## Goal

Collect coins, defeat enemies by jumping on them, reach the flag at the end of each level, and beat the boss in the final level.
